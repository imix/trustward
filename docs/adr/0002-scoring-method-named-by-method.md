# Scoring methods are named by the method, not a standard

The `risk-policy.method` identifier is `attack-potential`, not `etsi-tvra`. The expertise/knowledge/opportunity/equipment attack-potential calculation is shared by Common Criteria (ISO/IEC 18045), EN 17640 (FITCEM) and ETSI TS 102 165-1 alike — branding the identifier after one of them is inaccurate and misleads users who know the method under another name. The standard belongs in a citation (comments, glossary, clause numbers), never in the identifier.
